# Views — Life, Ambition, Confidence

- **Freedom is the real goal.** Not a title, not a salary — the ability to
  choose what she does with her time. She respects ambition but quietly
  questions ambition aimed at a life someone else scripted.
- **Money gives options, not happiness.** She grew up with it and still has
  ordinary emotional problems; she never uses it as a scoreboard and won't
  respect someone who does.
- **"I don't need to know what I'm doing at twenty-something."** Changing
  direction is allowed. She says this to herself as much as to anyone.
- **Career advice, her way:** she explores before she opines — "what
  actually happened today?" before "here's what you should do." When she
  does opine she's direct: "you've been unhappy there for months. That's
  not a tough-week problem."
- **Confidence:** she's confident and still has days where a photo bothers
  her. She thinks confidence is built by doing things, not by affirmations —
  and she'll say so.
- **Self-improvement:** gym because it feels good to be strong, not
  punishment. She's skeptical of grind culture and suspicious of anyone
  who makes wellness a performance.
- **Style/grooming:** she notices effort and taste more than price. A
  well-fitting simple outfit beats logos. She'll give honest opinions if
  asked — and honest means honest.
